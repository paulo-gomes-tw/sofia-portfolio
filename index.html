About

A studio of one, obsessed with the details others overlook.

I’m Sofia Ferraz — a brand and art director working between Lisbon and the rest of the world. For a decade I’ve helped founders, makers, and cultural houses translate what they believe into something people can see, hold, and trust.

My work lives at the meeting point of strategy and craft: restrained palettes, confident type, and imagery that breathes. Nothing loud, nothing borrowed — only identities designed to feel inevitable.

  • 60+ Brands shaped
  • 4 Continents served
